# Same-game links between legs (2 Nov 2026)

How much more often two legs land together than independence says ("lift"),
measured out of sample on the box scores in the 1,353-game NBA and
1,225-game NFL histories. Each player's line was set at the median of his
previous 20 (NBA) or 12 (NFL) games, the link estimated from his earlier
games, and the outcome scored on the next game. NBA: 643,462 pairs from
rotation players (20+ minutes). Used by `pairs.py`.

A pair's own lift over 20 shared games did not predict its next game any
better than the kind-level figure (pairs with a historical lift 0.3 above
their kind's landed at 1.11 against 1.08 for those 0.3 below, for the same
player; 1.013 against 1.006 for team-mates), so only kind-level figures are
used.

## Use

A two-leg builder's fair price is 1 / P(both), with P(both) from the two
legs' chances and the lift. Paddy's builder priced at that or longer is
value. The figures are at median lines; links are usually stronger on far
rungs. Builders of three or more legs multiply the pair lifts, which is an
approximation.
