Fair and Need are what the evidence implies, not what anyone is offering. For a football team leg that evidence includes team strength (Elo as of kick-off, shown under the selection) as well as the record; a player leg's record is pulled towards a coin flip by as much as past picks show it should be. Need is the price the evidence supports once the sample size is paid for, and it is the one to bet off. Neither includes the bookmaker's margin, which runs 5–8% a market and compounds with every leg you add.
