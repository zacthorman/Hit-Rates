Why multiples are worse than their legs A multiple is worse than its legs at a bookmaker's price. The margin compounds with every leg, and the shop sells accumulators because they are the most profitable thing on the counter. Legs in the same game can move together, so the same-game builders above are priced from how often their legs landed together, not by multiplying. Legs in different games do not move together: in the site's own published legs the correlation between them is effectively nil, and replayed over past matches, multiplied prices landed about as often as they said (1.01 times). So multiplying is right for the cross-game prices, and they are not optimistic on that account. Any error in them comes from the legs themselves.
